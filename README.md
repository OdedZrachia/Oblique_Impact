# Oblique Impact: Pardies and Mariotte

Interactive reconstructions of oblique-impact constructions in
Ignace-Gaston Pardies’s *Discours du mouvement local* (1670)
and Edme Mariotte’s *Traitté de la percussion* (1684).
These correspond to Figures 4 and 5 in Oded Zrachia’s PhD
dissertation on the French Jesuit Gabriel Daniel
(Tel Aviv University, in preparation).

## Interactive figures

- [Figure 4 — Pardies](https://odedzrachia.github.io/Oblique_Impact/fig4.html)
- [Figure 5 — Mariotte](https://odedzrachia.github.io/Oblique_Impact/fig5.html)

Both figures allow the angle of impact to be varied and include
an animation and an explanation of the construction.

Figure 4 illustrates how, under Pardies’s rules, the sum of
the bodies’ speeds increases while “respective motion” is conserved.

Figure 5 presents two cases of oblique impact treated by Mariotte:
one between equal bodies and another in which the struck body is
three times as heavy as the striker. Its calculator reckons each
body’s quantity of motion as weight multiplied by speed and adds
these quantities without regard to direction.

## Historical sources

- Pardies, *Discours du mouvement local* (1670), §§28–31;
  upper-left diagram on the plate marked “§ 28. page 68.”
- Mariotte, *Traitté de la percussion*, third edition (1684),
  Part II, proposition III, pp. 213–17, fig. 22.

The diagrams are simplified reconstructions. The interactive controls,
animations, colours, and numerical calculator are explanatory additions.
Assumptions and conventions are stated on each figure’s page.

## Opening the figures locally

Download the repository and open `fig4.html` or `fig5.html`
in a web browser. Keep `jsxgraphcore.js` in the same folder
as the HTML files. No installation or build step is required.
