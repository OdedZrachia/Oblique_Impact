Oblique Impact: Pardies and Mariotte

Interactive reconstructions of oblique-impact constructions in Ignace-Gaston Pardies’s Discours du mouvement local (1670) and Edme Mariotte’s Traitté de la percussion (1684).

These correspond to Figures 4 and 5 in Oded Zrachia’s PhD dissertation on the French Jesuit Gabriel Daniel (Tel Aviv University, in preparation).
